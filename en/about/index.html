About Me

A thousand years have passed; the whipping steed, the eastern cliff, the poem remains.

Self-introduction does not come naturally to me; pinning labels on oneself only exposes one's rough edges. But my thinking has shifted: reserved modesty fits poorly in a restless age, and only those who dare to present themselves can carve out a foothold. Perhaps only when you embrace the world will the world embrace you in return.
